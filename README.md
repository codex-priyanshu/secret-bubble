## 🌟 What Makes Secret-Bubble Unique?

| Traditional Chat Apps (WhatsApp / Telegram) | 🛡️ Secret-Bubble (Telegram Pro) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Locks the entire app or whole chat thread. | **Only sensitive/private messages are masked**; general public chats remain readable. |
| If someone takes your unlocked phone, they can read all open chats. | **Intimate & feelings messages stay blurred/locked** behind your Fingerprint / Face ID even inside open chats. |
| You have to manually remember to lock things. | **Built-in AI Auto-Shield** automatically detects adult/intimate/romantic talks and auto-locks them. |

## 🚀 Key Features

* **📱 Telegram-Style Homepage Layout:**
  * **Pinned Top Channel:** 🌍 **Global Public Channel** pinned at the top for community discussions.
  * **🤖 Meta AI Assistant:** Always pinned and ready to assist with real-time AI responses.
  * **Categorized Tabs:** Filter between *All Chats*, *Direct Messages*, *Public Channel*, and *Bots*.
  * **Telegram Message Bubbles:** Delivery double checkmarks (`✓✓`), live views counter (`👁️ 15`), and emoji reactions.

* **🤖 Integrated Meta AI Assistant Bot:**
  * Direct 1-on-1 conversations with Meta AI.
  * Multilingual AI reasoning across English, Hindi, and Hinglish.
  * Answers questions, explains privacy protocols, translates messages, and assists with coding.

* **🎭 Granular Message-Level Biometric Protection:**
  * Normal messages appear clear, while sensitive/intimate talks appear as frosted biometric cards: *"🔒 Private Message - Tap with Fingerprint / Face ID"*.

* **🔥 Disappearing / Self-Destructing Messages:**
  * Set message burn timers (10s, 30s, 1m, 1h). Messages automatically self-delete upon expiration.

* **🔒 Passcode Screen Lock:**
  * 1-Click Telegram top padlock icon locks the entire app screen with a 4-digit passcode or biometric verification.

* **👁️ Anti-Shoulder Surfing Shield:**
  * Automatically covers the chat screen with a frosted privacy shield whenever you switch tabs or your window loses focus.

* **🤖 AI Privacy Auto-Shield (Multilingual NLP):**
  * Auto-detects sensitive topics in **English, Hindi, and Hinglish**:
    * 🔞 **Adult & Physical Intimacy:** Sex, physical relations, bedroom talks, body references.
    * ❤️ **Romance & Feelings:** Love, crush, emotional affection, miss you.
    * 🔒 **Secrets & Confidential:** Passwords, OTPs, "kisi ko mat batana", confidential info.

* **🔐 Real User Registration & Login:**
  * Secure username & password authentication with custom profile pictures and DP upload.
